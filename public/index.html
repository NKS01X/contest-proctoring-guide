Contest Proctoring participant guide

Download the app. Connect your handle.

Contest Proctoring is a desktop app that logs which windows and programs you use during a contest. Your host reviews the log in a dashboard. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux, and there is nothing to build. Setup takes about two minutes.

  1. Download the app from the registration website.
  2. Open the app and note the 5-character session code at the top.
  3. Connect your Codeforces handle in the app and on the website.
  4. Keep the app open until the contest ends.

Set up step by step

  1. Go to the registration website. Open nks01x.github.io/contest-proctoring and download the app for your computer. It works on Windows, macOS and Linux.
  2. Open the app. At the top it shows a 5-character session code. Keep the app open. The code is case-sensitive, so copy it exactly.
  3. Connect in the app. Type your Codeforces handle exactly as it appears on your profile and click Connect.
  4. Connect on the website. Back on the registration website, enter the same Codeforces handle and the session code from the app, then click Connect.
  5. Keep the app open until the contest ends.

If you connect the wrong handle, connect again with the right one and tell your host.

What is recorded

These events are documented, and nothing else:

EventWhat it means
Active window changeEach time you switch to another window, the app logs its title.
Program start or stopEach time a process starts or stops on your computer, the app logs its name.
FlagIf a window or program is on the host's blacklist, the event is marked as flagged.

Events are saved on your computer and uploaded every 10 seconds. If your internet drops, they stay saved and upload when you are back online.

If you want to know about anything else, or what is on the blacklist, ask your host.

During the contest

  • Keep the app open the whole time. Closing it stops recording, so your host may see a gap.
  • Close programs you do not need before the contest starts. Anything on the blacklist gets flagged when it starts or comes to the front.
  • A short internet drop is fine. Nothing is lost while you are offline.

Windows Defender warning

On Windows, Defender may flag the app because it reads window titles and the list of running programs. This is expected. If it blocks the app, add an exclusion for the contest and remove it afterwards.

  1. Open Windows Security, then Virus & threat protection.
  2. Under Virus & threat protection settings, choose Manage settings.
  3. Scroll to Exclusions and choose Add or remove exclusions.
  4. Add the app's file or folder.

Questions

Connect does nothing or fails

Check that the app is open, your handle is spelled correctly, and the 5-character session code matches the app exactly, including capital letters. If it still fails, ask your host.

Do I need to build or install anything else?

No. Download the app from the registration website and open it.

Does it work on Mac and Linux?

Yes. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux.

Where is my session code?

At the top of the app.

I closed the app by accident

Open it again right away and check that your handle still shows as connected. Tell your host about the gap.

Who can see my data?

The host sees it in their dashboard, protected by a host key. Ask your host how long they keep it.
