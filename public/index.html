Contest Proctoring participant guide

Keep the app open while you compete.

Contest Proctoring is a desktop app that logs which windows and programs you use during a contest. Your host reviews the log in a dashboard. Setup takes about two minutes.

  1. Launch the app. It starts recording straight away.
  2. Enter your Codeforces handle and click Connect. This tells the host which session is yours.
  3. Leave it open until the contest ends.

Get the app

Your host gives you the app, or tells you where to download it. If they point you to the source code instead, jump to Build it yourself.

Install and launch it before the contest starts, so you have time to fix anything. The app shows a session token on its main screen. You do not need to copy it unless your host asks.

Connect your handle

  1. Open the app.
  2. Type your Codeforces handle exactly as it appears on your profile.
  3. Click Connect.

Once connected, your host sees your handle next to your session. If you connect the wrong handle, connect again with the right one and tell your host.

There is also an optional web portal where you can enter your handle and session token. Use it only if your host says so.

What is recorded

The project README lists these events and nothing else:

EventWhat it means
Active window changeEach time you switch to another window, the app logs its title.
Program start or stopEach time a process starts or stops on your computer, the app logs its name.
FlagIf a window or program is on the host's blacklist, the event is marked as flagged.

Events are saved on your computer and uploaded every 10 seconds. If your internet drops, they stay saved and upload when you are back online.

This guide covers what the README documents. If you want to know about anything else, or what is on the blacklist, ask your host.

During the contest

  • Keep the app open the whole time. Closing it stops recording, so your host may see a gap.
  • Close programs you do not need before the contest starts. Anything on the blacklist gets flagged when it starts or comes to the front.
  • A short internet drop is fine. Nothing is lost while you are offline.
  • Check the app once after the contest starts to confirm your handle shows as connected.

Windows Defender warning

Windows Defender may flag the app because it reads window titles and the list of running programs. This is expected. If it blocks the app, add an exclusion for the contest and remove it afterwards.

  1. Open Windows Security, then Virus & threat protection.
  2. Under Virus & threat protection settings, choose Manage settings.
  3. Scroll to Exclusions and choose Add or remove exclusions.
  4. Add the app's file or folder.

Build it yourself

Only needed if your host gave you the source code. You need Go 1.23 or newer and the Wails CLI.

git clone https://github.com/NKS01X/contest-proctoring
cd contest-proctoring/daemon
go mod tidy
wails build

The built app appears in daemon/build/bin. From the repo root, ./comp.sh does the same in one step.

The app talks to a default server. If your host runs their own, they will give you a BACKEND_URL value to set before you launch it.

Questions

Connect does nothing or fails

Check your internet connection and the spelling of your handle. If it still fails, ask your host whether their server is running.

Does it work on Mac and Linux?

The README calls it cross-platform. The only platform note it gives is for Windows.

Where is my session token?

On the app's main screen.

I closed the app by accident

Open it again and check that your handle still shows as connected. Tell your host about the gap.

Who can see my data?

The host sees it in their dashboard, protected by a host key. Ask your host how long they keep it. The backend can delete old events automatically.
